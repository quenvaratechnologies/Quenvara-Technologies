# Brand Name Report — Quenvara Technology

**Status: researched, NOT legally cleared.** Nothing here is a claim that the name is globally unique or available.
No domain was bought, no trademark filed, no company registered.

## Final name
**Quenvara Technology** (display name is editable in Admin → Settings → Company; the logo/letter mark and favicon follow it).

## Meaning and concept
An invented word. "Quen" (quest/query, inquiry) + "vara" (a soft, Latin/Sanskrit-flavoured ending suggesting direction and craft). Reads as "the craft of answering hard questions" — fitting for an engineering partner. Easy to pronounce (KWEN-vah-rah), spell and remember; no dictionary meaning in English or Urdu to my knowledge (not exhaustively checked in other languages).

## Candidates considered (10+)
Quenvara, Vorentis, Zephyrion, Nexaryn, Lumivance, Calvyra, Tervanox, Orbivance, Kaldyra, Stratavane, Veloraxis, Aurevant.
Each was screened with web searches; Quenvara was kept as the finalist because it showed no identical software/IT company and the least overlap. Detailed per-candidate results for the rejected names were not recorded, so treat their rejection as a judgement call, not a documented finding.

## Search findings (web search only)
- Exact "Quenvara": no software/IT company found. Only unrelated fantasy-RPG content ("Quenvara the Rootmother").
- Similar spellings/sounds with existing marks or businesses: QUENARA, CENVARA, QVARA, QUINAVO, QENTURA, Quinova. These are near-neighbours and should be reviewed by a trademark attorney for confusing similarity.
